# Zusha for Claude: privacy notice

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

This notice covers the Sabra Zusha plugin for Claude and its hosted MCP server at
`https://mcp.daily.knafaim.app/mcp`. Shalom Maman operates Sabra. The broader
[Sabra privacy policy](https://daily.knafaim.app/privacy) covers your Sabra
account and the main application. Questions or data requests can be sent through
[Sabra support](https://daily.knafaim.app/support).

## What the connection sends

When you use a plugin tool, Claude sends the tool name and the arguments needed
for that request to Sabra's MCP server. Sabra returns the result to Claude, so
Anthropic can process it as part of your Claude conversation under
[Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Public
learning and calendar tools can be used without a Sabra account. Personal
tools require you to connect your own Sabra account through OAuth and consent
to the requested access. The plugin package itself contains no account tokens
or local data store.

Depending on the tool you call and the access you grant, arguments and results
can include a city or location for zmanim or minyanim, your study plan and
progress, reminders, quiz history, and community information. Tools that
change your Sabra data require the relevant server permission and an explicit
approval step. Installing the plugin does not grant administrative access.

## Storage and retention

Sabra stores account information and changes made through the connection in
the same account data store used by its main application. This can include
your account identifier and sign-in details, study progress and preferences,
reminders, quiz results, and community participation. Account data is kept
while the account is active and is deleted when you delete the account, subject
to the limited legal or fraud-prevention exceptions in Sabra's privacy policy.

The MCP server does not store your Claude conversation transcript or a location
supplied only for a tool request. To limit abuse, it may keep an IP address in
memory for up to one hour. Its operational logs record the tool name, status,
response time, a random request identifier, and server version; they do not
record request content. Anthropic's retention of your Claude conversation and
tool results is governed by its own privacy policy and account settings.

## Your choices

You can stop using the plugin or disconnect its connector in Claude. You can
request access to, correction of, or deletion of Sabra account information
through [Sabra's account deletion page](https://daily.knafaim.app/delete-account)
or [support](https://daily.knafaim.app/support). Disconnecting Claude does not
delete your Sabra account. Sabra does not sell personal information or use
advertising in this version of the service.
